Who We Are

About Timi Akorede Literacy Center

TLC is a premier educational institution committed to nurturing literate, confident, and excellent learners who thrive in an ever-changing world.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional literacy education that equips every learner with the knowledge, skills, and values needed to excel academically and contribute meaningfully to society.

Our Vision

To be a leading center of literacy excellence, recognized for producing well-rounded graduates who embody academic brilliance, integrity, and lifelong learning.

Our Values

Excellence, Integrity, Innovation, Respect, and Collaboration form the cornerstone of everything we do — from classroom to community.
